Original Compositions

Over the course of his career, Weekes has written over twenty original compositions. Here are those most recently recorded.

Circe’s Wisdom

Inspired by Madeline Miller’s Circe, Circe’s Wisdom reflects the gradual transformation of one of Greek mythology’s most compelling figures. Rather than portraying power alone, the composition traces Circe’s growth through solitude, motherhood, and time, seeking to musically express the wisdom she acquires over the course of her life.

梅花 (Winter’s Plum Blossom)

Winter’s Plum Blossom was inspired by the Chinese proverb 宝剑锋从磨砺出，梅花香自苦寒来—“A sharp sword is forged through grinding; the fragrance of plum blossoms comes from the bitter cold.” Reflecting on the idea that beauty and strength emerge through adversity, the work explores resilience, perseverance, and quiet hope.

Biography

Toronto-born pianist, composer, educator, and producer Micah Weekes creates music that invites listeners into places. Drawing from classical music, modern jazz, gospel, R&B, and quiet acoustic traditions, his work is shaped by a fascination with how music can evoke landscapes, architecture, memory, and story. Whether through improvisation, composition, or collaborative performance, he seeks moments of stillness—music that lingers rather than demands attention.

Weekes’ musical voice is the meeting point of several lifelong influences. He began studying piano at the Suzuki School of Music, where he was immersed in the works of Beethoven, Mozart, Schumann, and the classical repertoire. At the same time, years of playing in church from childhood fostered a lasting connection to gospel music, contemporary worship, and the rich harmonic language of hymns. His artistic vocabulary expanded through the JazzFM Youth Big Band, while artists such as Ichiko Aoba, J Dilla, and Walter Smith III continue to shape his sense of texture, rhythm, and atmosphere. Together, these influences have led to a musical language that is both classically informed and deeply rooted in the traditions of jazz, gospel, and R&B.

As both a performer and composer, Weekes is drawn to music that tells stories without words. His original works often begin with an image, a place, or an idea, unfolding through melody and improvisation into quiet, reflective narratives. This same sensibility informs his collaborative performances, particularly in voice-and-piano interpretations of hymns and acoustic repertoire. A recipient of a full scholarship to the University of Toronto Faculty of Music, where he completed a Bachelor of Music in Piano Performance, Weekes has shared the stage with artists including Terri Lyne Carrington and Rich Brown and received multiple composition awards from the Ontario Registered Music Teachers’ Association. He is currently exploring the organ as both a performance instrument and a new creative voice, while collaborating with fellow musicians throughout Ontario to write and perform music rooted in contemporary gospel and R&B.
